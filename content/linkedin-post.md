Most AI shopping assistants have zero memory. Every session starts from scratch, forcing users to re-enter their budgets, dietary constraints, and preferences.

We solved this in MaNa Angadi by integrating Vectorize Hindsight as a persistent agent memory layer.

Now, when a customer mentions their ₹10k wedding budget and preference for authentic GI-tagged Pochampally silk in Session 1, Hindsight retains it. When they return in Session 2 asking for gift ideas, the agent automatically recalls those exact constraints—zero repetitive prompting required.

Built with React 18, Express, TypeScript & Hindsight.

Explore the architecture & test suite on GitHub: https://github.com/thatikonda0522/mana-angadi
